### 1. Angola: the peat nobody counted (Angolan Highlands Water Tower)
- Seems: a remote, little-mapped plateau in Angola; really: the source of the Okavango, Zambezi and Cuanza rivers holds far more buried carbon than assumed, mapped with Sentinel-2 plus ground cores (the "water tower" framing is from the source, check wording).
- Fact: peatland probability map of the 380,382 km2 Angolan Highlands Water Tower: 8,662 km2 high certainty, 11,167 km2 lower certainty (2.28-2.94% of the area); 51 ground-verified peat sites from five expeditions 2019-2023; all verified sites fell inside predicted zones (mean probability 85.7%). Mongabay quotes carbon dating back 11,000 years.
- Primary: Lourenco M.C. et al., "A probabilistic map of tropical peatlands in the Angolan Highlands Water Tower", Phil. Trans. R. Soc. B 381(1956), DOI 10.1098/rstb.2024.0481, 20 Aug 2026, CC BY 4.0. https://doi.org/10.1098/rstb.2024.0481 (via Crossref api.crossref.org/works/10.1098/rstb.2024.0481). Read: ABSTRACT (Crossref) + Mongabay lead/body https://news.mongabay.com/2026/08/angolas-peatlands-highly-underestimated-new-study-suggests/
- Why now: published 6 weeks ago; open access (licence clear for figures); Angola is the gap country.
- SHOW: map built from the paper's own probability layer (ask authors / check supplementary data; if not downloadable, we cannot claim a "real run"); diagram of a peat core and why a drained peat does not re-sequester; real images only if CC BY figures from the paper. Mine-clearance (Halo Trust) angle named in source.
- Format: long film (8-10 min) or Short on "the 11,000-year carbon sponge".
- Risk: low. "Water tower" and carbon-store numbers need paper check; Mongabay quote on carbon age is a secondary source; threats paragraph (burning) edges into land-use politics, keep neutral.

### 2. Angola: the 15th-century graves under a future reservoir (Caculo Cabaca)
- Seems: a dam-rescue dig; really: Angola's oldest radiocarbon-dated burials, its only known ancient cremation, and the earliest dry-stone architecture in central Angola, from decades before the Portuguese arrived (early 1400s per phys.org). Pairs with, but is distinct from, the Aquiry earthworks story.
- Fact: 117 clusters of stone structures recorded, 65 excavated, 6 tumuli with human remains; radiocarbon dates from burial contexts; funerary, domestic and iron-production activity interleaved on the middle Cuanza.
- Primary: D'Oliveira S.M.S. et al., "First Evidence of Burial Practices and Stone Constructions from the 15th Century a.d. Pre-Colonial Middle Cuanza River, Angola", J. Field Archaeology 51(4), 2026, DOI 10.1080/00934690.2026.2623354 (tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/00934690.2026.2623354, access paywalled in search result). Read: ABSTRACT via search result + LEAD/body phys.org 26 Jul 2026 https://phys.org/news/2026-07-angola-oldest-burials-ancient-cremation.html (a phys.org page, summarised).
- Why now: published July 2026; also 19 Sep 2026 Egypt-Angola agreement to train Angolan archaeologists (allAfrica, search snippet only, not opened).
- SHOW: map of the Cuanza and the reservoir, plan diagram of a tumulus from the paper (licence: figures credited "D'Oliveira et al. 2026"; closed-access paper, so ask authors/journal for permission or redraw from described data), real images only after permission.
- Format: Short, or paired with the Aquiry film as a long.
- Risk: low; paywalled primary, so the producer must get the PDF; dam name not given in the phys.org summary, find it; "oldest burials" is the authors' claim and needs an exact qualifier ("oldest dated").

### 3. Mozambique: 654 Stone Age sites, 272 new, mapped by a Portuguese-led team

### 5. Brazil: the Sirius holder that films a solar cell while it works (CNPEM/LNLS)
- Seems: a lab fixture; really: a 200 nm X-ray beam on Brazil's Sirius synchrotron (Carnauba beamline, Taruma station) can watch a perovskite solar cell's atoms, chemistry and electronics change while it is heated and lit.

### 7. Brazil: USP storm-tracker TITAN-LSTM (30-minute radar nowcast)

- Format: Short.
- Risk: medium (fast-moving "AI" claims; accuracy index "0.2 to 0.7" is not defined in the lead).

- Primary: arXiv:2607.07834, submitted 8 Jul 2026 (Bnoussaad, Cherif, Pinto, Neves, Silva, Bernardino) https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.07834. Read: ABSTRACT (summary of the abs page). 5,882 observations over nine shellfish zones.

- Format: Short.
- Risk: medium: it is a preprint (not peer reviewed), and the topic touches food safety (borderline medical, we would only describe the monitoring, not advice).
