## What "an end-screen section" means here

The template is defined in `growth-kit-proposal.md` (section 3). In short: after the film's last line and its sound have finished, one section of **16 seconds** in which the left half of the frame is kept empty for YouTube's "next film" element, a small circle is kept empty for the Subscribe element, the credits sit in a column on the right, the film's own music runs underneath to a resolved low ending, and the narrator says **one sentence** naming the next film and why. It replaces today's credits card (5 to 14.5 s in the current cuts), so the net added length is 2 to 11 seconds.

**Cost of adding it to a film:** one narration line (seconds on server 2), the section built from the kit (the first film pays for the template; the rest reuse it), music extended under it by the sound specialist, **one encode and one independent review** of the last 20 seconds plus `sr-audiocheck` and `sr-margin`. For a film already validated, that is the price; for a film that has an encode pending anyway, it rides on that encode.

**Open or not** follows the coordinator's board: films in "ajustes" or "produção" can change; films in "avaliação" cannot without the coordinator's decision.
